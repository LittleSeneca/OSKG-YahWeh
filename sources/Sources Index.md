## Source Quality Framework

When evaluating sources, I track:

- **Scholarly vs. popular** — Is this peer-reviewed or general audience?
- **Consensus vs. fringe** — Does this represent mainstream scholarship?
- **Dated vs. current** — Has the field moved since this was published?
- **Confessional vs. critical** — Does the author have theological commitments that shape their analysis?
