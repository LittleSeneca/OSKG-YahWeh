# Primary Source Texts and Translations: Key Inscriptions for Early Israelite Religion & Yahweh

Compiled on 2026-07-22

---

## 1. KUNTILLET AJRUD INSCRIPTIONS (c. 800 BCE)

**Location:** Kuntillet Ajrud (Horvat Teman), northeast Sinai Peninsula
**Date:** Late 9th / early 8th century BCE (carbon-14: 801-770 BCE)
**Discovery:** Excavated by Ze'ev Meshel, 1975-76
**Medium:** Inscriptions on pottery (pithoi) and wall plaster
**Script:** Paleo-Hebrew / Phoenician script

---

### Inscription 3.1 (Pithos A) — "Yahweh of Samaria and his Asherah"

**Paleo-Hebrew text (reconstructed):**
  )mr )$yw hm[lk] )mr lyhlyw wlyw($h wl[...] brkt )tkm lyhwh $mrn wl)$rth

**English Translation (scholarly consensus):**
"Says )A$iyaw the king: Say to Yahēliyaw, and to Yaw(āsah, and to [...] I bless you all by Yahweh of Samaria and by his Asherah."

**Key terms:**
- *yhwh $mrn* — "Yahweh of Samaria" (Yahweh associated with a specific location)
- *)$rth* — "his Asherah" (the goddess Asherah, or a cult object, with possessive suffix)

---

### Inscription 3.6 (Pithos B) — "Yahweh of Teman and his Asherah"

**Paleo-Hebrew text (reconstructed, multi-line):**
  (1) )mr
  (2) )mryw )-
  (3) mrl )dny
  (4) h$lm )t
  (5) brktk ly-
  (6) hwh tmn
  (7) wl)$rth yb-
  (8) rk wy$mrk
  (9) wyhy (m )dn-
  (10) y )rk .hym
  (11) b$lm )mr h)

**English Translation (scholarly consensus):**
(1) Says (2) )Amaryaw: ") (3) MRL, my lord, (4) is all well with you? (5) I bless you to Ya- (6) hweh of Teman (7) and to his Asherah. May he bl- (8) ess you and protect you, (9) and may he be with my lo- (10) rd as a long life (11) in peace." says he.

**Key terms:**
- *yhwh tmn* — "Yahweh of Teman" (Teman = southern region/Edom)
- *wl)$rth* — "and to his Asherah"
- Formulaic epistolary blessing structure

---

### Wall Plaster Inscriptions

Fragmentary plaster inscriptions also found, including partial references to *yhwh* (Yahweh), *)l* (El), and theophoric names.

**Significance:** These inscriptions provide the earliest extrabiblical evidence of Yahweh paired with Asherah, suggesting that in popular Israelite religion of the 9th-8th centuries BCE, Asherah was worshipped as Yahweh's consort. The references to "Yahweh of Samaria" and "Yahweh of Teman" indicate multiple localized manifestations of Yahweh.

## 2. KHIRBET EL-QOM INSCRIPTION (c. 750-700 BCE)

**Location:** Khirbet el-Qom (biblical Maqqedah?), West Bank, territory of ancient Judah (between Lachish and Hebron)
**Date:** Second half of 8th century BCE
**Discovery:** Excavated by William G. Dever, 1967 (Tomb 2)
**Medium:** Stone tomb inscription
**Script:** Paleo-Hebrew

---

### The Uriyahu Inscription (Tomb 2)

**Paleo-Hebrew text (reconstructed):**
  )ryhw h($r ktbh
  brk )ryhw lyhwh
  w[m.sryh l])$rth hw$( lh
  l)nyhw

(Reading debated, especially line 3)

**English Translation (scholarly consensus, Dever and others):**
"Uriyahu the honourable has written this.
Blessed is/be Uriyahu by Yahweh,
And from his oppressors by his asherah he has saved him.
By Oniyahu."

Alternative rendering:
"Uriyahu the rich has caused it to be written:
Blessed be Uriyahu to Yahweh;
And from his enemies, by his Asherah, he has delivered him.
— By Oniyahu."

**Key features:**
- *lyhwh* — "by/to Yahweh" (no place name qualifier, unlike Kuntillet Ajrud)
- *l)$rth* — "by/to his Asherah" (same formulation as Kuntillet Ajrud)
- Associated with a "magic hand" symbol (apotropaic)
- Unlike Kuntillet Ajrud, no geographic epithet for Yahweh (suggesting post-fall of Samaria date)

**Significance:** Confirms the pattern seen at Kuntillet Ajrud — Yahweh worshipped alongside Asherah in 8th century Judah. The absence of a place name for Yahweh may reflect the post-722 BCE situation after the fall of the Northern Kingdom.

## 3. DEUTERONOMY 32:8-9 + 4QDeut (Textual Variant)

**Book:** Deuteronomy 32:8-9 (Song of Moses)
**Date:** Poem likely composed 10th-8th century BCE; Qumran manuscript c. 1st century BCE

---

### TEXTUAL TRADITIONS COMPARED

| Tradition | Deut 32:8b (key variant) | Deut 32:9 |
|-----------|--------------------------|-----------|
| **Masoretic Text (MT)** | *lemispar bene yisra'el* — "according to the number of the **sons of Israel**" | "For YHWH's portion is his people, Jacob his allotted inheritance." |
| **Septuagint (LXX)** | kata arithmon angelon theou — "according to the number of the **angels of God**" | Same as MT |
| **4QDeut-j (Qumran)** | *lemispar bene 'el* (or *bene 'elohim*) — "according to the number of the **sons of God**" | Same as MT |

---

### English Translation (with the earliest recoverable reading):

**Deut 32:8-9 (Reconstructed Original):**
8 When **Elyon** (the Most High) apportioned the nations,
  when he divided humankind,
  he fixed the boundaries of the peoples
  according to the number of the **sons of God** (*bene 'el*).
9 For **YHWH**'s portion is his people,
  Jacob his allotted inheritance.

---

### Commentary on the Variant:

The Masoretic Text reads *bene yisra'el* ("sons of Israel"), but this is widely regarded by scholars as a later theological "correction." The earliest recoverable reading, preserved in 4QDeut-j and reflected in the Septuagint (which translates as "angels of God"), is *bene 'el* or *bene 'elohim* ("sons of God").

**Why this matters:**

1. **Two deities in view:** The passage distinguishes between *Elyon* (the Most High) who distributes the nations, and *YHWH* who receives Israel as his portion. This reflects an older polytheistic/pre-monotheistic cosmology.

2. **Divine council context:** Each nation gets a "son of God" (a lesser deity from the divine council) as its patron. YHWH is Israel's patron deity within Elyon's allotment.

3. **Theological correction:** Later scribes, uncomfortable with the polytheistic implications, changed "sons of God" to "sons of Israel" — a reading that makes little sense contextually.

4. **Scholarly consensus:** Most critical scholars (including Robert Alter, Mark S. Smith, Michael Heiser, and the editors of the NRSV) accept "sons of God" as the original reading.

---

### Other DSS variants in the same passage (4QDeut-q on verse 43):

**MT (Deut 32:43):**
"Sing aloud, O you nations, of His people; for He avenges the blood of His servants..."

**4QDeut-q + LXX:**
"Rejoice, O heavens, together with Him; and bow down to Him all you **gods**, for He will avenge the blood of His **sons**..."

This variant also preserves a more polytheistic original — calling on "gods" to worship YHWH, and referring to divine "sons" rather than human "servants."

## 4. SOLEB SHASU INSCRIPTION (c. 1400 BCE)

**Location:** Temple of Soleb, Nubia (modern Sudan), built by Amenhotep III
**Also:** Amarah West, Nubia (copy by Ramesses II, 13th century BCE)
**Date:** 14th century BCE (Amenhotep III, r. 1390-1353 BCE)
**Language:** Egyptian hieroglyphs
**Medium:** Column base inscriptions listing Egypt's enemies

---

### Hieroglyphic Rendering

The name appears in a list of Shasu (nomadic) territories:

  tA SAsw yhwA

**Gardiner sign list:** N16 (tA) = "land", M8 (SA) = pool with lotus, M23 (sw) = sedge, phonetic complements w-w-y-h-h-V4-wA-G1-A

---

### Translation:

**"The Land of the Shasu of YhwA"** (or "Yahweh-land of the Shasu")

Variant rendering: *tA SASw yhw* — "The Land of the Shasu: Yhw"

---

### The Amarah West List (Seti I / Ramesses II, 13th century BCE)

Six Shasu territories are listed on column bases at Amarah West, Nubia:
1. *s'rr* — Seir (Edom)
2. *rbn* — Laban/Labanah
3. *sm't* — possibly related to the Simeonites
4. *wrbr* — possibly Wadi Bar
5. ***yhw*** — **Yahweh**
6. *pysps* — unknown

---

### Scholarly Significance:

1. **Earliest possible reference to Yahweh:** Michael Astour observed that "the hieroglyphic rendering corresponds very precisely to the Hebrew Tetragrammaton YHWH, or Yahweh, and antedates the hitherto oldest occurrence of that divine name — on the Mesha Stele — by over five hundred years."

2. **Yahweh's southern origins:** The Shasu were nomadic pastoralists from the Edom/Midian/Seir region. This aligns with biblical traditions that Yahweh "came forth from Seir" (Judges 5:4, Deuteronomy 33:2, Habakkuk 3:3).

3. **Toponym or theonym:** *yhwA* could be a place name ("Yahweh-land") or a divine name. Most scholars see it as a toponym derived from the divine name — a region named after the god worshipped there.

4. **Pre-Israelite Yahweh worship:** K. Van Der Toorn concludes: "By the 14th century BC, before the cult of Yahweh had reached Israel, groups of Edomites and Midianites worshipped Yahweh as their god."

5. **The Kenite/Midianite Hypothesis:** This inscription supports the theory that Yahweh worship originated among the Midianites/Kenites of the southern Transjordan and was later adopted by early Israel.

## 5. MERNEPTAH STELE (c. 1208 BCE)

**Also known as:** The Israel Stele, Victory Stele of Merneptah
**Pharaoh:** Merneptah (r. 1213-1203 BCE), son of Ramesses II
**Date:** c. 1208 BCE (Year 5 of Merneptah)
**Discovery:** Flinders Petrie at Thebes, 1896
**Current location:** Egyptian Museum, Cairo
**Medium:** Black granite stele (reused back of Amenhotep III stele)
**Language:** Egyptian hieroglyphs, 28 lines

---

### The Canaan/Israel Section (Lines 26-28)

**Egyptian Hieroglyphs (transliteration of line 27 — the Israel line):**

  *Y.si.ri.A-r | fk.t | bn | pr.t | =f*
  Ysriar | feket | ben | peret | -ef
  "Israel | is laid waste | [negative] | seed/grain | his/its"

---

### English Translation (Lines 26-28, scholarly consensus):

The princes are prostrate, saying: "Peace!"
Not one raises his head among the Nine Bows.
Desolation is for Tjehenu (Libya);
**Hatti** is pacified;
Plundered is the **Canaan** with every evil;
Carried off is **Ashkelon**;
Seized upon is **Gezer**;
**Yanoam** is made non-existent;
**Israel is laid waste — its seed is no more;**
**Kharru** (Hurru/Syria) has become a widow because of Egypt.
All lands together are pacified.
Everyone who was restless has been bound.

---

### Key Epigraphic Details:

1. **Determinatives are crucial:** The hieroglyphic writing uses different determinatives (classifying signs):
   - *Canaan* — determinative for **foreign land**
   - *Ashkelon, Gezer, Yanoam* — determinative for **city/town**
   - ***Israel*** — determinative for **a people/ethnic group** (man + woman + plural strokes) — NOT a land or city

   This tells us that in the Egyptian perception of c. 1208 BCE, **Israel was understood as a people group, not a settled state or territory** — consistent with a semi-nomadic or recently settled tribal population.

2. **"Its seed is no more"** — Typical Egyptian rhetorical hyperbole. "Seed" (*prt*) could mean literal grain/offspring; this is a standard victory boast, not a literal report of genocide.

3. **Merneptah's campaign:** The stele primarily records a Libyan war. The Canaan campaign may be a brief summary of an earlier action, or a propagandistic claim extending to Egypt's sphere of influence.

---

### Significance:

- **Earliest mention of "Israel" outside the Bible** — and the only mention of Israel in any ancient Egyptian text.
- **Dated to the late 13th century BCE** — this is the *terminus ad quem*: Israel as a recognizable entity existed in Canaan by 1208 BCE.
- **Israel as a people, not a state** — the determinative confirms an early-stage tribal/ethnic entity.
- Together with the Mesha Stele, Tel Dan Stele, and Kurkh Monoliths, one of only four Iron Age inscriptions mentioning Israel by name.
