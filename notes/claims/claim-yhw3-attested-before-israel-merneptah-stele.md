# fleming-ybi-2.7: The Egyptian evidence is "before Israel" — Yhw3 of the Shasu predates Israel in the historical record

**Source:** [[Fleming — Ch 2 — Yhwʒ of Shasu-Land]] — Daniel Fleming, *Yahweh Before Israel* (2021), Chapter 2

## The Claim

"Yhw3 is attested before Israel, which is not a name linked to the Shasu. As encountered by Egypt, the Israel people from the Merneptah Stele (ca. 1207) and the Yhw3 group of the Shasu (early 14th century) have nothing to do with each other" (Fleming, p. 85).

## Evidence

- The Merneptah Stele uses a people-determinative for Israel (man + woman + plural strokes)
- Israel was encountered in Canaan; Yhw3 was encountered somewhere in Shasu-land
- No Egyptian text connects the two names
- Two centuries separate the attestations

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** This is simply factual: the Egyptian record attests Yhw3 (c. 1400) before Israel (c. 1207), and they're separate entries in separate contexts.

## Stakes

This establishes the chronological priority of the name Yahweh over the name Israel in the historical record. Yahweh really was "before Israel" — not just theologically but chronologically, in the oldest available evidence.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Na'aman (2011), who "conflates Israel with the Shasu category" (p. 85 n.90). Some scholars argue the early Israelites WERE Shasu.

**Alternative reading:** Israel may have emerged FROM Shasu populations, so the chronological separation is artificial — Israel and Yhw3 are phases of the same underlying reality.

## Assessment

Factually solid but interpretively limited. The names are separate in the Egyptian record, but that doesn't mean the populations were separate — it means Egyptian scribes used different categories at different times for different purposes. Fleming acknowledges this complexity (p. 85: "groups identified with Yahweh and with Israel may have been affiliated or closely aligned").
