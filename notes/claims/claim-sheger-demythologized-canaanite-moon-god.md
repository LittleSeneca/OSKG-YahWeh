# day-ygc-6.4: Sheger is a demythologized Canaanite moon god

**Source:** [[Day — Chapter 6 — Yahweh and the Astral Deities]] — John Day, *Yahweh and the Gods and Goddesses of Canaan* (2000), Chapter 6

## The Claim

"Just as in the Deuteronomistic references *ʻaštārôt*... is a demythologization of the goddess Astarte, so it is clear that *šeger*... is the demythologization of a god Sheger" (p. 166). New evidence proves Sheger was a moon god.

## Evidence

1. The phrase *šegar ʼălāpêkā wĕʻaštĕrōt ṣōʼnekā* (Deut 7:13; 28:4, 18, 51) = "the increase of your cattle and the young of your sheep" — both terms are demythologized deities
2. Punic personal name *ʻbdšgr* "servant of Sheger" — long suspected
3. Ugaritic attestation (KTU² 1.148.31): *šgr* mentioned alongside *ʻṯtrt* as receiving offerings
4. Deir ʻAlla (Balaam text): *šgr* and *ʻštr* paired — exactly matching the OT collocation
5. **NEW: Sheger identified as moon god** — hieroglyphic Hittite correspondences (d30 = *ša-ga-ra/i*), and at Emar the 15th day of the month (full moon) was sacred to Shaggar (p. 166)
6. **Crescent = bull's horn:** "It is probable that the connection between Sheger, the moon god, and the Old Testament's use of the name in connection with the herd, lies in the crescent moon's looking like a bull's horn" (p. 166-167)

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** The Emar and Hittite evidence for Sheger as moon god is recent and strong; the parallel with Astarte/Astartes-of-sheep makes the case intuitive.

## Stakes

This is a parallel case to Astarte/Astartes-of-sheep — the OT preserves the names of Canaanite deities fossilized in agricultural vocabulary, long after the deities themselves were forgotten. It's evidence of how thoroughly Canaanite religion permeated Israelite daily life and language.
