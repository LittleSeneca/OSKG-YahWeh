# lewis-ocg-6.6: Yahweh was a southern storm/warrior deity clothed in northern Canaanite cultural garments

**Source:** [[Lewis — Chapter 6 — Origin of Yahweh]] — Theodore J. Lewis, *The Origin and Character of God* (2020), Chapter 6

## The Claim

"Yahweh's origin seems to be southern, yet many of the garments put onto Yahweh are sewn from northern and inland Canaanite cloth." The complete absence of Yahweh from Ugaritic texts despite hundreds of deities is the strongest argument against northern origins. Yet Yahweh fights the same foes as Baal (Yam, Leviathan, Mot), rides the clouds like Baal, and inherits El's epithets and divine council. "We simply do not know the social settings and mechanisms by which the widespread pre-Israelite Canaanite lore... was woven on to the god Yahweh of southern, southeastern, and Transjordanian fame."

## Evidence

Northern elements: Ugaritic combat myths (Baal vs. Yam/Leviathan → Yahweh vs. Sea/Leviathan), El's epithets (Elyon, Olam, Shadday, Creator), divine council, sacrificial vocabulary, poetic conventions, stock type-scenes. Southern elements: archaic poetry (Judg 5:4-5, Deut 33:2, Hab 3:3-7, Ps 68:8-9) consistently locating Yahweh in Seir/Edom/Teman/Paran/Sinai/Midian; Kuntillet ʿAjrud inscriptions mentioning "Yahweh of Teman/the Southland"; Shasu texts; Cross's Reuben hypothesis.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** Both northern and southern evidence strands are independently strong; only the mechanism of merger is unknown (the Propp Principle applies — we have perhaps 5% of the data).

## Stakes

The entire nature of Israelite religion. If Yahweh is essentially a southern storm god who absorbed Canaanite mythology, Israelite religion is a creative synthesis, not a unique revelation. The distinctiveness is in the CONFIGURATION (southern warrior + Canaanite high god + ethical demands + aniconism), not in any single element.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Northern-origin-only scholars (Leuenberger: Yahweh "belongs to the North Levantine-Syriac type of the kingly weather-god Adad-Baal"). Kaufmann and confessional scholars who reject the comparative framework entirely.

**Alternative reading:** The southern poetry could be a late literary fiction — a "southernizing" of Yahweh to connect him to Sinai traditions. But Lewis argues this is unlikely given the persistence of Sinai in the face of Zion theology's tendency to absorb everything.

## Assessment

This is where Lewis lands and it's the most defensible position. The evidence for southern origins (archaic poetry + Shasu + Kuntillet ʿAjrud) is strong. The evidence for northern cultural clothing (Ugaritic parallels) is overwhelming. The mystery is how they merged — and that may be permanently lost.
