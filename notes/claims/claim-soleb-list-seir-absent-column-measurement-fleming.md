# fleming-ybi-summary.1: Seir was probably NOT in the original Amenhotep III Soleb list

**Source:** [[Fleming — Book Summary]] — Daniel E. Fleming, *Yahweh Before Israel* (2021)

## The Claim

Fleming argues that the Egyptian toponym "Seir" (often restored in the Soleb Shasu list as tꜣ šꜣśw sʿrr) was probably NOT in the original Amenhotep III Soleb list. The column N4 measurement argument is the key: the column has room for only 9 names arranged as 4+5, not the 10 names (6+4) that the Seir restoration requires. Without Seir, the sole geographical anchor for Yhwʒ in the Egyptian evidence disappears — the Shasu-land names are kinship-based people-groups (mhwt = "families"), not towns or sanctuaries. Yhwʒ is a people, not a place and not a god.

## Evidence

- Column N4 on the Soleb list has space for 9 names in a 4+5 arrangement, not 10 names in a 6+4 arrangement as the Seir restoration would require
- The Shasu-land names are designated as mhwt ("families"), indicating kinship-based people-groups, not fixed geographical locations
- The Ramses II Amara list (which was likely copied from Soleb) shows signs of later elaboration — the Seir link may be a 13th-century addition, not original 14th-century data

## Confidence

**Rating:** medium

**Rationale:** Fleming acknowledges this as a genuinely debatable argument that needs Egyptological verification. The column measurement is specific and falsifiable but hasn't been independently confirmed.

## Stakes

If Seir was not in the Soleb list, the primary geographical argument for Yahweh's southern (Edom/Seir) origin collapses. The entire Midianite/Kenite Hypothesis loses its earliest external evidence. Without a geographical anchor, Yhwʒ floats free as a people-name unattached to any specific location — which is precisely Fleming's argument.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Thomas Römer, Mark S. Smith, Frank Moore Cross, and essentially all defenders of the Midianite Hypothesis who rely on the Soleb-Seir connection as primary evidence for Yahweh's southern origin. [[claim-kenite-midianite-hypothesis-best-explanation]] and [[claim-yhwh-southern-desert-origin-soleb-poetry-synthesis]] depend on Seir being in the Soleb list.

**Alternative reading:** The standard reading (Giveon, Astour, Redford) restores Seir in the Soleb list, placing Yhwʒ in the Edom/Seir region. Cross and Römer both treat this as settled.
