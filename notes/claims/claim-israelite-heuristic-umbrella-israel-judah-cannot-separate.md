# lewis-ocg-3.2: "Israelite" Is a Heuristic Umbrella; Israel and Judah Cannot Be Cleanly Separated

**Source:** [[Lewis — Chapter 3 — Methodology]] — Theodore J. Lewis, *The Origin and Character of God* (2020), Chapter 3

## The Claim

"By following the convention of using the name 'Israelite' as an umbrella term, I do not mean to homogenize distinctly different Israelite and Judean religions into a unified whole. The impossibility of so doing should be obvious." But he adds: "Our textual sources are a nightmare for the historian of religion who thinks she/he can separate northern/Israelite traditions from texts... that have been transmitted, reimagined, and even reworked by southern/Judean hands."

## Evidence

- Lewis quotes Daniel Fleming: "All of [the Bible's] contents have been filtered through Judahite assumptions"
- Schniedewind's sociolinguistic argument: northern dialect features were "collected, edited, and [re-]written in Jerusalem and Judah" using standardized Judean Hebrew
- Fleming's 2012 attempt to recover "the legacy of Israel in Judah's Bible" — methodological hurdles described as "high"
- The biblical texts we have are the product of Judahite scribal culture, even when they preserve northern traditions

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** High that the term is heuristic and that northern/southern religions differed; medium-high that they cannot be separated at all — Fleming and Römer have made some progress.

## Stakes

Whether we can ever know what northern Israelite religion looked like, or whether everything we think we know is actually Judahite retrojection. This matters enormously for questions like: Did the northern kingdom worship El separately from Yahweh? Was the golden calf at Bethel an El symbol or a Yahweh symbol?

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Fleming would say more can be recovered than Lewis admits. Römer uses the Elijah-Elisha cycles and Hosea to reconstruct northern Yahwism. The maximalist school would argue the biblical picture of northern apostasy is basically accurate.

**Alternative reading:** Fleming's work on "the legacy of Israel in Judah's Bible" suggests more can be recovered than Lewis concedes. Römer's reconstruction of northern cult (bull imagery, local manifestations of Yahweh) shows productive use of filtered sources.

## Assessment
