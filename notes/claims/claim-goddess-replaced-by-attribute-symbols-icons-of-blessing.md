# keel-ggi-4.3: The Goddess Replaced by Attribute Symbols — the Mechanism of the Aniconic Turn

**Source:** [[Keel Uehlinger — Part IV — Iron Age IIA]] — Keel & Uehlinger, *Gods, Goddesses, and Images of God in Ancient Israel* (1998), Part IV

## The Claim

The anthropomorphic naked goddess of the Bronze Age is "substituted by the entities through which she worked" — suckling mother animals, caprids at trees, scorpions, and branches. The symbols become autonomous, detached from any specific goddess identity. "Most often, only the incarnated form of the blessings she was providing is portrayed" — the suckling mother animal alone, caprid alone, tree alone. This is the mechanism by which the goddess becomes the asherah (cultic symbol). The same power that was depicted anthropomorphically in the Bronze Age is now represented by her attributes.

## Evidence

- The stylized palmetto tree flanked by caprids (the substitute symbol for Asherah) appears on the Taanach cultic stands, a Megiddo terra-cotta plaque (Str. V), and numerous seals
- The tree-caprids constellation blends "two different themes: the constellation of the 'Mistress of the Caprids' with the caprids at the tree"
- "Substitute entities, such as a horse or a suckling mother animal, can then be worshiped in her place"
- "Sometimes, only a scorpion placed with the caprid, or a branch, or a worshiping human even give a hint that the goddess is really in the background behind these substitute creatures or activities"
- Terra-cotta figurines of women holding tambourines replace the naked goddess — "Just as Judges 4-5 describe Deborah as a 'mother in Israel,' replacing the warrior goddess, perhaps Anat... so also the portrayal of the female tambourine player replaces the iconographic representations of the goddess in Israel"
- The clothed "woman with disk" figurines: the disk is a tambourine (not a solar disk or sacrificial cake), identifying the figure as a human cult participant, not a goddess

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** The pattern of goddess → tree/caprid/lion/nursing-animal is documented across multiple media and sites. The tambourine player interpretation is well-argued, and the iconographic continuity from LB goddess contexts to Iron IIA abstracted symbols is too strong for the "generic symbol" reading.

## Stakes

This is the mechanism by which the goddess becomes the asherah (cultic symbol). The worshipers may still experience her as a personal divine presence, but the official iconography has abstracted her into a tree. This is how "his asherah" can function as BOTH a cultic symbol AND carry the goddess's meaning — the symbol IS the goddess, made transparent. Deuteronomy 28:4's "blessed shall be the šagar of your cattle and the 'aštārōt of your flock" shows the goddess's name becoming a common noun for fertility blessing.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Scholars who interpret the tambourine-holding women as goddesses (Amiran's solar goddess theory). Also those who see the tree-caprids motif as an independent symbol unrelated to goddess traditions.

**Alternative reading:** The tree and nursing animals could be generic fertility/blessing symbols, not substitutes for a specific goddess. The "substitution" framing may overstate the connection to Asherah specifically.

## Assessment

The iconographic continuity is too strong for the "generic symbol" reading. The same constellation (tree + caprids + lions) has clear goddess associations in LB contexts. In Iron IIA, the goddess figure disappears from the constellation — but the constellation persists. This is abstraction, not replacement. The goddess is still there — she is just become transparent.
