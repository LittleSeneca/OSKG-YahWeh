# sommer-bog-3.3: P's *kavod* theology rejects the fluidity model

**Source:** [[Sommer — Chapter 3 — Rejection of Fluidity]] — Benjamin Sommer, *The Bodies of God* (2009), Chapter 3

## The Claim

The Priestly (P) source replaced the fluidity model with a different hypostatic substitute: the *kavod* (Glory). The *kavod* is not God's body — it's a radiance that represents God's presence without BEING God.

## Evidence

**Key texts:**
- Exodus 24:15-17: "The *kavod* of Yhwh dwelt on Mount Sinai... the appearance of the *kavod* of Yhwh was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain"
- Exodus 40:34-35: "The cloud covered the Tent of Meeting and the *kavod* of Yhwh filled the Tabernacle"

**The kavod is an emergency measure:**
- God in P is dangerous — "stunningly bright, so that it had to be surrounded by dark clouds to protect anyone nearby"
- The *kavod* is not God's body — it's a radiance that represents God's presence without BEING God
- P never depicts God walking, eating, or appearing in human form
- P's creation account (Genesis 1) has God speaking things into existence from a distance — contrast J's hands-on God in Genesis 2

**What P rejected:** The material anthropomorphism of J/E. P's God has no body humans could see — only the blinding *kavod* that must be screened by cloud.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high

**Rationale:** P never depicts God walking, eating, or appearing in human form; the *kavod* consistently functions as a screen between the dangerous divine presence and human observers.

## Stakes

P's *kavod* theology is the second step in the anti-fluid revolution. Where D's solution was the Name, P's solution was the Glory. Both are hypostatic substitutes — they do the work of divine presence without requiring God to actually be present in a body. P's God is even more remote than D's: mediated by cloud, fire, and radiance.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** [[claim-biblical-god-has-body-standard-notion]] — Sommer's own intro claim that God has a body is partially contradicted by P's theology

**Alternative reading:** The *kavod* IS God's body in P — a body of light/fire rather than flesh. The radiance is not a screen but the divine form itself.

## Assessment

P's theological revolution is more radical than D's. D's God is still corporeal — just located exclusively in heaven. P's God is screened behind cloud and fire — the *kavod* is not a body but a controlled emission of dangerous radiance. This is the halfway point between J/E's embodied God and the fully transcendent God of later Judaism.
