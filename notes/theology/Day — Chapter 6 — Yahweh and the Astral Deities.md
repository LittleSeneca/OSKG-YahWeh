# Day — Chapter 6: Yahweh and the Astral Deities (Sun, Moon, and Lucifer)

Day examines the astral cult in Israel. His key arguments: the sun and moon worship in Judah was native Canaanite, not Assyrian import; Yahweh was NOT equated with the sun (contra a growing scholarly trend); Samson is a solar hero behind the legend; Sheger is a demythologized Canaanite moon god; and the famous "Lucifer" passage (Isa 14:12-15) reflects a Canaanite myth about Athtar/Venus attempting to usurp Baal's throne on Mt Zaphon, applied polemically to Nebuchadnezzar's destruction of Jerusalem.

---

## Claim 1: The sun cult in Judah was native Canaanite, not Assyrian import
→ [[claim-sun-cult-judah-native-canaanite-not-assyrian-import]] | **day-ygc-6.1** | Confidence: high
  Pre-Assyrian archaeological evidence (Taanach cult stand 10th c. BCE, Hazor horse's head c. 925 BCE) plus abundant sun-related place names (Beth-Shemesh, En-Shemesh, Timnath-heres) prove the sun cult was indigenous. Josiah's reform targeted all Canaanite cults — sun worship was no exception.
  Supports: [[claim-yahweh-not-equated-with-sun-entourage-not-identification]] (native sun cult was separate from Yahwism, not equated)
  Contradicted by: Spieckermann, Oestreicher, Gressmann — Assyrian import thesis

---

## Claim 2: Yahweh was NOT equated with the sun (contra Taylor, Stähli, Niehr, Keel/Uehlinger)
→ [[claim-yahweh-not-equated-with-sun-entourage-not-identification]] | **day-ygc-6.2** | Confidence: medium-high
  No Yahweh-Šemeš theophoric names exist — a striking absence if Yahweh was solar (cf. Yahweh-Baal names like Bealiah). The sun was worshipped in Yahweh's temple as part of the host of heaven (divine council), not as Yahweh himself. Ps 84:12 "sun and shield" is better read "rampart and shield." The "host of heaven = sons of God" framework from Chapter 1 provides the model: sun as subordinate entourage, not identification.
  Depends on: [[claim-yahweh-el-originally-distinct-deities]] (host of heaven = divine council framework)
  Contradicted by: J.G. Taylor (1993), Keel/Uehlinger — solar Yahwism thesis

---

## Claim 3: Samson is a solar hero behind the Israelite legend
→ [[claim-samson-solar-hero-behind-israelite-legend]] | **day-ygc-6.3** | Confidence: low-medium
  Samson's name (*Šimšôn* = "sunny") and his cultic geography (all locations within 6 miles of Beth-Shemesh) are suggestive. Ps 19:5-6: the sun as "strong man" (*gibbôr*) mirrors Samson's character. Seven locks = sun's rays; Delilah (*delîlâ*) = *laylâ* "night." Day appropriately hedges: this is the weakest claim in the chapter.
  Depends on: [[claim-sun-cult-judah-native-canaanite-not-assyrian-import]] (native solar cult required for solar hero), [[claim-day-baal-dying-rising-hosea-isaiah-daniel-resurrection-chain]] (same demythologization pattern)

---

## Claim 4: Sheger is a demythologized Canaanite moon god (parallel to Astarte in "Astartes of your sheep")
→ [[claim-sheger-demythologized-canaanite-moon-god]] | **day-ygc-6.4** | Confidence: high
  *Šegar ʼălāpêkā* ("increase of your cattle") is a demythologized moon god — parallel to *ʻaštĕrōt ṣōʼnekā* ("Astartes of your sheep"). Emar evidence: full moon day sacred to Shaggar. Hittite hieroglyphic: d30 = *ša-ga-ra/i*. Crescent moon = bull's horn explains the cattle association. Clean and convincing.
  Depends on: [[claim-ashtoreth-vocalization-boshet-deliberate-scribal-distortion]] (Astarte/Astartes demythologization provides the parallel framework)

---

## Claim 5: Isa 14:12-15 ("Lucifer") reflects a Canaanite myth of Athtar/Venus attempting to usurp Baal's throne on Mt Zaphon — applied to Nebuchadnezzar's destruction of Jerusalem in 586 BCE
→ [[claim-isaiah-14-lucifer-venus-athtar-canaanite-myth-nebuchadnezzar]] | **day-ygc-6.5** | Confidence: medium-high
  *Hêlēl ben šāḥar* = morning star Venus = Canaanite Athtar. NOT the same as Ugaritic Athtar myth (appointed, not prideful; descends, not cast down), but a variant. NOT Ezek 28 (human figure, initially perfect). Day's original proposal: Nebuchadnezzar's destruction of Zion's temple = attack on heavenly Zaphon, since Zaphon = Zion (Ps 48:3). Jeremiah 50-51 strengthens the connection. The "Lucifer"/Satan tradition is a remythologization of a Canaanite astral myth reused for anti-Babylonian polemic.
  Depends on: [[claim-day-mt-zaphon-baal-transferred-yahweh-heaven-zion]] (Zaphon applied to Zion), [[claim-yahweh-el-originally-distinct-deities]] (Jebusite Elyon mediation), [[claim-day-chaoskampf-canaanite-baal-yam-not-babylonian-marduk-tiamat]] (Baal cycle framework)

## Chapter 6 Overall Assessment

| Claim | Confidence | Most Vulnerable To |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| Sun cult = Canaanite, not Assyrian | HIGH | Spieckermann's Assyrian thesis (weakened by pre-Assyrian archaeology) |
| Yahweh NOT equated with sun | MEDIUM-HIGH | Taylor's book-length counter-argument (Day engages but dismisses quickly) |
| Samson = solar hero | LOW-MEDIUM | Thin evidence; clever wordplay, not primary data |
| Sheger = demythologized moon god | HIGH | Emar/Hittite evidence is recent and strong |
| Isa 14:12-15 = Venus/Athtar myth → Nebuchadnezzar/586 BCE | HIGH (Venus); MEDIUM-HIGH (Athtar); MEDIUM (586 BCE specific) | Novel proposal; needs more testing against alternative historical identifications |

**Strongest section:** The Isa 14:12-15 analysis. The multi-stage argument (Venus identification → Athtar myth → distinction from Ezek 28/Ps 82 → Jebusite mediation → Nebuchadnezzar application) is Day's most original contribution in the chapter.

**Weakest section:** The Samson-as-solar-hero theory. Day appropriately hedges, but some of the evidence is the kind of punning and pattern-seeking that can generate false positives.

**Most important implicit contribution:** Day demonstrates that the "host of heaven" framework (sun, moon, stars as Yahweh's subordinate entourage = sons of God from Chapter 1) provides a coherent model for how astral worship could coexist with Yahwism without equating Yahweh with the astral bodies. This is the Catholic "veneration not worship" analogy he explicitly makes (p. 159).
