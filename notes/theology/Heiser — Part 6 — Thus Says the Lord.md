# Heiser — Part 6: Thus Says the Lord (Chapters 26-30)

## Overview: The Prophets Within the Deuteronomy 32 Worldview

Heiser argues that the prophets operated within the cosmic-geographical framework of Deuteronomy 32. Their oracles against the nations were not just political commentary — they were declarations that Yahweh was coming to judge the corrupt *elohim* behind those nations. "The Day of the Lord" is the day Yahweh reclaims the nations from their rebellious divine administrators.
