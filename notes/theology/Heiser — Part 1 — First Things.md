# Heiser — Part 1: First Things (Chapters 1-2)

## Chapter 1: Reading Your Bible Again for the First Time

### Claim: Psalm 82 broke Heiser's theological filter — and should break yours too
→ [[claim-psalm-82-describes-divine-council-not-human-judges]] | **heiser-ur-1.1** | Confidence: high
  Psalm 82 describes a divine council where Yahweh judges other *elohim* — the evangelical "human judges" reading fails exegetically. Heiser's watershed moment: the plural *elohim* reading is the scholarly consensus, matching ANE divine council parallels word-for-word. Supports: [[claim-psalm-82-yahweh-prosecutes-gods-injustice]], [[claim-divine-council-seventy-sons-of-el]], [[claim-deut-32-8-9-sons-of-el]].

### Claim: We must read the Bible through ancient eyes, not modern filters
→ [[claim-bible-must-be-read-through-ane-eyes-not-modern-filters]] | **heiser-ur-2.1** | Confidence: high
