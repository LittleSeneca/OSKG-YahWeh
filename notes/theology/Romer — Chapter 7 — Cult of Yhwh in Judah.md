# Römer, The Invention of God — Chapter 7

## The Cult of Yhwh in Judah

---

## Claim 1: Jerusalem was not the only Yahwistic sanctuary — Judah had multiple temples
→ [[claim-judah-multiple-yahwistic-sanctuaries-arad-lachish]] | **romer-inv-7.1** | Confidence: high
  Archaeological evidence at Arad, Lachish, and Beer-Sheba proves multiple Yahwistic sanctuaries existed in Judah. The Deuteronomistic ideal of exclusive Jerusalem worship was not historical reality — it was imposed by reforming kings.
  Depends on: [[claim-yhwh-worshipped-diverse-local-manifestations]]. Supports: [[claim-centralization-worship-jerusalem-late-political-project]].

---

## Claim 2: Yhwh was not Jerusalem's original god — he rose to supremacy gradually, competing with a solar deity
→ [[claim-yhwh-not-jerusalem-original-god-solar-competition]] | **romer-inv-7.2** | Confidence: high
  Yhwh competed with Jerusalem's older solar deity and absorbed El Elyon's identity. The Melchizedek episode (Genesis 14) preserves Jerusalem's El heritage; Joshua 10 may preserve the solar competition. Fits a regional pattern (Moab, Ammon).
  Depends on: [[claim-israel-name-el-originally-el-worshippers]], [[claim-david-brought-yhwh-to-jerusalem-political]].

---

## Claim 3: Centralization of worship in Jerusalem was a late political project
→ [[claim-centralization-worship-jerusalem-late-political-project]] | **romer-inv-7.3** | Confidence: high
  Centralization (Deuteronomy 12) was a 7th-century innovation. The 1 Kings 3:2 footnote acknowledges it was an innovation that needed explaining. Hezekiah and Josiah pursued political consolidation as much as religious reform.
  Depends on: [[claim-judah-multiple-yahwistic-sanctuaries-arad-lachish]]. Supports: [[claim-biblical-pure-yahwism-deuteronomistic-propaganda]].

**Key takeaway:** The Jerusalem-centered, exclusive Yahwism of the Bible was not the original religion of Judah. It was imposed by reforming kings — especially Josiah — who destroyed their own people's traditional sanctuaries. What the Bible presents as "restoring pure religion" was actually "suppressing traditional religion."
