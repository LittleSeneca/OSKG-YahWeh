# Heiser — Part 3: Divine Transgressions (Chapters 10-15)

## Genesis 6:1-4 and the Watchers

### Claim: The "sons of God" in Genesis 6 were divine beings who produced the Nephilim
→ [[claim-genesis-6-sons-of-god-divine-beings-nephilim-demigods]] | **heiser-ur-10.1** | Confidence: high
  *Bĕnê hā'ĕlōhîm* always means divine beings in the Hebrew Bible. The Septuagint translates as "angels." Second Temple literature unanimously reads it as angels. The Sethite interpretation is a 3rd-century CE apologetic invention. Supports: [[claim-divine-council-seventy-sons-of-el]], [[claim-deut-32-8-9-sons-of-el]].
