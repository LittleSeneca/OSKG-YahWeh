# Heiser — Parts 7-8: Kingdom Already and Kingdom Not Yet (Chapters 31-40+)

## Part 7: The Kingdom Already

### Claim: Jesus's ministry was about reclaiming the nations from the corrupt *elohim*

**Heiser's framework:** Jesus didn't just come to save individual souls. He came to reclaim the nations that had been assigned to corrupt divine beings at Babel. His exorcisms were demonstrations of authority over the *elohim*. His Great Commission ("make disciples of all nations") was a reversal of Babel — taking the nations BACK from the corrupt *elohim*.

**Evidence:**
- Matthew 28:18: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me" — Jesus now has the authority the *elohim* had abused
- Mark 5:1-20: The Gerasene demoniac — "Legion" (Roman military term) in Gentile territory — Jesus exercising authority over territorial spirits
- Acts 2: Pentecost reverses Babel: instead of dividing languages, the Spirit unites nations under one message

**The cosmic reading of Paul:**
- Ephesians 3:10: "so that through the church the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places"
- Ephesians 6:12: "we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers"
- Colossians 2:15: Christ "disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame"

**Confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH on Paul's cosmic language (it's explicitly about spiritual powers). MEDIUM on Heiser's specific reading of the exorcisms and Pentecost.

## Part 8: The Kingdom Not Yet

### Claim: The final restoration involves the *elohim* being replaced by glorified humans

**Heiser's framework:** Psalm 82 promises that the corrupt *elohim* will "die like men." Who replaces them? Glorified believers. The "we will judge angels" language (1 Cor 6:3) means believers will take the administrative roles in the divine council that the fallen *elohim* forfeited. Humanity was always destined for this — "a little lower than *elohim*" (Psalm 8:5, reading *elohim* not "angels").

**The endgame:**
- Revelation 2:26-27: "The one who conquers... I will give authority over the nations"
- Revelation 3:21: "The one who conquers, I will grant him to sit with me on my throne"
- The divine council is reconstituted — not with fallen *elohim* but with redeemed humans serving alongside Yahweh

**Confidence:** LOW-MEDIUM. This is creative theological synthesis. Whether Paul, John, and the psalmist intended Heiser's specific framework is debatable.

## Heiser's Grand Narrative

| Stage | Event | Cosmic Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| **Creation** | God creates heaven, earth, divine council | Hierarchy: Yahweh > *elohim* > humans |
| **Eden** | Serpent (*nāḥāš*) rebels | First divine transgression |
| **Babel** | Nations assigned to *elohim* | Administrative structure established |
| **Abraham** | Israel = Yahweh's direct portion | Counter-nation begins |
| **Genesis 6** | *Elohim* father Nephilim | Second divine transgression |
| **Flood** | Judgment on corrupted humanity | Reset |
| **Conquest** | Canaan reclaimed | Corrupt *elohim* displaced |
| **Exile** | Israel fails its mission | Judgment on Yahweh's portion |
| **Jesus** | Reclaims authority | *Elohim* judged (Psalm 82 fulfilled) |
| **Church** | Nations discipled | Babel reversed |
| **New Creation** | Believers join the council | Humans elevated to *elohim* status |

**Heiser's contribution:** He provides the most comprehensive single-framework reading of the entire Bible available from an evangelical perspective. Whether it's TRUE depends heavily on whether his key interpretive move — Elyon=Yahweh in Deut 32:8-9 — holds. If it doesn't, the bottom falls out of the cosmic geography. Yahweh was never the Most High distributing nations. He was one of the nations' recipients. And the entire framework is built on a mistaken identification.
