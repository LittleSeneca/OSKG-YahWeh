# Römer, The Invention of God — Chapter 4

## How Did Yhwh Become the God of Israel?

---

## Claim 1: The name "Israel" is an El name, not a Yhwh name — the group originally venerated El
→ [[claim-israel-name-el-originally-el-worshippers]] | **romer-inv-4.1** | Confidence: high
  "Israel" contains the theophoric element *'el* — it's an El name regardless of which etymology is correct. Romer prefers "May El reign" (from *s-r-r*), supported by a Masoretic revision at Hos 12:5 that conceals the original meaning. The biblical folk etymology "struggles with God" (from *s-r-h*) supplanted the original when Yhwh the warrior god replaced El. "If Yhwh had been the original god, the group would be named something like *Yisra-yahu*."
  Key edges: Depends on [[claim-yahweh-el-originally-distinct-deities]] and [[claim-el-original-god-israel-yahweh-imported-later]]. Supports [[claim-yahweh-southern-storm-warrior-origin]] and [[claim-divine-council-seventy-sons-of-el]].

## Claim 2: The Merneptah Stele (c. 1210-1205 BCE) is the first extra-biblical mention of Israel — as an ethnic group, not a kingdom
→ [[claim-merneptah-stele-first-mention-israel-ethnic-group]] | **romer-inv-4.2** | Confidence: very-high
  Universally accepted. The stele's determinative marks Israel as an ethnic group (man + woman + plural strokes), not a city or territory. Located in Ephraim by geographic sequence. "Israel is destroyed, its seed is no longer" — the first historical mention of God's people is a boast about wiping them out. No indication they worshipped Yhwh at this point.
  Key edges: Supports [[claim-israel-name-el-originally-el-worshippers]] (Israel existed as named group by 1200 BCE) and [[claim-biblical-texts-acknowledge-yhwh-chose-israel-later]] (historical anchor for Israel before Yhwh).

## Claim 3: The biblical texts themselves acknowledge that Yhwh and Israel were not originally connected
→ [[claim-biblical-texts-acknowledge-yhwh-chose-israel-later]] | **romer-inv-4.3** | Confidence: high
  Hosea 9:10 ("I found Israel like grapes in the desert"), Ezekiel 20 ("I chose Israel in Egypt"), and Exodus 6:2-3 (patriarchs didn't know the name Yhwh) all describe the Yhwh-Israel relationship as having a beginning. The texts disagree on WHERE it began but agree that it began. The Bible's own narrative admits Yhwh was adopted.
  Key edges: Depends on [[claim-israel-name-el-originally-el-worshippers]] (El's people predate Yhwh). Supports [[claim-bible-acknowledges-yhwh-foreign-origin]] (roman-inv-1.4) and [[claim-el-original-god-israel-yahweh-imported-later]] (smith-ehg-1.1).

**Strongest section:** The Merneptah Stele analysis — detailed, precise, and grounded in universally accepted evidence.

**Weakest section:** The Masoretic revision hypothesis at Hosea 12:5 — ingenious but speculative. Could be right, could be wrong; there's no way to verify.

**The narrative arc so far:** Chapter 2 established Yhwh's southern origin. Chapter 3 proposed Midianite mediation. Chapter 4 now shows that "Israel" was originally an El-worshipping group, not a Yhwh-worshipping one. Yhwh was a latecomer to a people who already had a god.
