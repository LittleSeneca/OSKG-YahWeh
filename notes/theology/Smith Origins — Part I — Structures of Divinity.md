# Smith — Part I: The Structures of Divinity (Chapters 1-4)

---

## Chapter 1: Anthropomorphic Deities and Divine Monsters

### Claim: ANE deities were structured spatially — benevolent gods at the center, monsters at the periphery

**Smith's claim:** Ugaritic and Israelite cosmology conceived of divine space as concentric circles. At the center: anthropomorphic benevolent deities (El, Baal, Yahweh) associated with cultivation, temples, and blessing. At the periphery: monstrous divine enemies (Yamm/Sea, Mot/Death, Leviathan) associated with chaos, the outback, and threat. "The center point of the cosmos is a garden on the god's holy mountain." The periphery is where monsters dwell.

**Evidence — Ugaritic cosmology:**
- Center: Baal's mountain Sapan — "pleasant place" (*n'm*), garden language, cultic blessing
- Home region: cultivated land, human habitation, benevolent deities
- Periphery: "rocks and brush" (*l'abnm wl'ṣm*), grazing land, dangerous forces
- Beyond: Yamm (Sea), Mot (Death), *tnn* (dragon) — no holy mountains, no cult

**Israel's transformation:** Israel INHERITS this spatial cosmology but transforms it. Yahweh absorbs Baal's center position. The monsters are demoted — from independent deities to created beings (Genesis 1:21: "God created the great sea monsters"). Psalm 74:13-14 has Yahweh crushing Leviathan's heads. "Israelite cosmic enemies tamed and denied."

**Confidence:** HIGH on the Ugaritic data. HIGH on Israel's transformation of the pattern.

## Chapter 2: The Divine Council

### Claim: The divine council had four tiers — and Israel inherited the full structure

**Smith's claim:** The Ugaritic divine council had four hierarchical tiers. Israel inherited this structure but progressively collapsed it as monotheism tightened.

**The four tiers:**
1. **Top tier:** El and Athirat (Asherah) — the divine patriarch and matriarch, the ultimate authority
2. **Second tier:** The major active deities — Baal, Anat, Yamm, Mot, Astarte, Shapshu — the "seventy sons of Athirat"
3. **Third tier:** Craftsman deities (Kothar-wa-Hasis), messenger deities (the *gpn w'ugr*), minor specialized gods
4. **Fourth tier:** The *il'ib* (divine ancestor), divinized royal dead (*rp'um*), household gods, messenger servants

**Israel's adaptation:**
- Tier 1: Absorbed into Yahweh — he becomes BOTH El and the active deity
- Tier 2: Demoted to angels/messengers or eliminated entirely
- Tier 3: Disappeared
- Tier 4: Transformed into the "host of heaven" or condemned as necromancy

**The biblical evidence:** Psalm 82 preserves the council structure (God judging the *elohim*). 1 Kings 22 shows the council in action (Micaiah's vision). Job 1-2 shows the *benê hā'ĕlohim* presenting themselves. "The language of council" survives even after the theology of the council is rejected.

**Confidence:** HIGH on the four-tier structure at Ugarit (textually demonstrable). HIGH on Israel's inheritance and transformation.

## Chapter 3: The Divine Family

### Claim: The pantheon was structured as a patriarchal household — and this IS the model for Israel's God

**Smith's claim:** "The royal patriarchal household as model for the pantheon" is the organizing metaphor of West Semitic religion. El is the divine patriarch. Athirat is the matriarch. The seventy sons are the royal household. This is not mythological fantasy — it's the projection of human social structure onto the cosmos. Israel inherited this model and its God progressively absorbed all family roles.

**The four tiers as household:**
- Tier 1: El (father) + Athirat (mother) = divine parents
- Tier 2: Baal and the seventy sons = the royal children, the active princes
- Tier 3: Craftsmen, messengers = household servants
- Tier 4: Ancestors = the household's dead

**The astral dimension:** "The astral background of El's family" — the seventy sons correspond to the seventy stars/gods of the night sky (cf. Deuteronomy 4:19, where the "host of heaven" are alloted to the nations). The stars ARE the divine family.

**Israel's transformation:** Yahweh absorbs El's patriarchal role. Asherah is first his consort, then eliminated. The seventy sons become the "host of heaven" — eventually demoted to angels, then to created celestial bodies. "The divine family was collapsed into a single figure."

**Confidence:** HIGH on the household model. HIGH on Israel's transformation.

## Chapter 4: Pluralities, Pairings, and Other Divine Relations

### Claim: Deities could overlap, pair, and merge — this conceptual flexibility enabled monotheism

**Smith's claim:** Ugaritic polytheism was not a fixed pantheon of discrete, non-overlapping deities. Gods could be paired (Baal and Resheph), identified (Astarte as the "Name of Baal"), and possess overlapping attributes. "The conceptual unity of Ugaritic polytheism" — its flexibility — made monotheism POSSIBLE. A rigid polytheism (like Greece) couldn't easily collapse into monotheism. A fluid polytheism (like Ugarit) could.

**The key conceptual moves:**
1. **Pairing:** Deities could function as a pair (Baal + Anat; Yahweh + Asherah)
2. **Overlap:** Divine qualities could be abstracted (Astarte as "name" of Baal = hypostasis)
3. **Absorption:** A god could absorb another's attributes (Baal absorbing Yamm's kingship)
4. **Extension:** Divine presence could extend into cult objects, places, and names

**Israel's use of this flexibility:** These four moves are precisely what Israelite religion did — pairing (Yahweh + Asherah), overlap (Yahweh's *kavod* as hypostasis), absorption (Yahweh absorbing Baal, El, the sun), and extension (Yahweh's Name in the temple). Monotheism was the logical EXTENSION of polytheistic flexibility, not a REVOLUTION against it.

**Confidence:** HIGH. This is Smith's most original argument. Monotheism wasn't Israel's unique invention — it was a possibility already present in Ugaritic polytheism that Israel realized more thoroughly than anyone else.
