# Heiser — Part 5: Conquest and Failure (Chapters 21-25)

---

## Claim 1: Law given in presence of divine council
→ [[claim-law-given-in-presence-divine-council-witnesses]] | **heiser-ur-21.1** | Confidence: high
  The LXX of Deut 33 reads divine beings at Sinai; Acts 7:53 and Heb 2:2 draw on this tradition. The law was delivered in a cosmic assembly, not a private mountain meeting. Supports: [[claim-divine-council-pervasive-biblical-reality-not-pagan-import]].

---

## Claim 2: Holiness = realm separation
→ [[claim-holiness-realm-separation-not-primarily-morality]] | **heiser-ur-22.1** | Confidence: medium-high
  Hebrew *qdš* means "set apart/distinct" — the purity laws are boundary markers between divine and mortal realms, not arbitrary moral rules. Depends on: [[claim-elohim-denotes-spiritual-realm-residence-not-divine-attributes]].

---

## Claim 3: Conquest targeted Nephilim bloodlines
→ [[claim-conquest-targeted-nephilim-bloodlines-not-ethnic-cleansing]] | **heiser-ur-23.1** | Confidence: medium
  The *ḥērem* commands target specific giant clans (Anakim, Rephaim, Zamzummim), not generic Canaanites. Goliath was a post-conquest Anakim survivor. Depends on: [[claim-genesis-6-sons-of-god-divine-beings-nephilim-demigods]].

---

## Claim 4: Serpent as divine throne guardian
→ [[claim-eden-serpent-divine-throne-guardian-not-animal]] | **heiser-ur-24.1** | Confidence: medium-high
  The *nāḥāš* was a divine throne guardian (seraph/cherub class) who rebelled, not a literal snake. Expands: [[claim-eden-serpent-nachash-divine-being-not-talking-snake]].

---

## Claim 5: *Ḥērem* as cosmic warfare
→ [[claim-herem-cosmic-warfare-reclaiming-territory-corrupt-elohim]] | **heiser-ur-25.1** | Confidence: low-medium
  Yahweh reclaiming nations from corrupt *elohim* via ritual destruction. Depends on: [[claim-babel-nations-assigned-to-divine-beings-deut-32-worldview]], [[claim-conquest-targeted-nephilim-bloodlines-not-ethnic-cleansing]]. Heiser's most strained argument.
