# Römer, The Invention of God — Chapter 8

## The Statue of Yhwh in Judah

---

## Claim 1: Yhwh was represented by standing stones (maṣṣēbôt) — these were divine images, not memorials
→ [[claim-yhwh-represented-by-standing-stones-massebot]] | **romer-inv-8.1** | Confidence: high
  Standing stones functioned as representations of Yhwh. The prohibition texts (Deut 16:22, Lev 26:1) lump *massebot* with "idols" — you don't ban what doesn't exist. The Arad temple had two steles in its holy of holies, probably Yhwh and Asherah — material evidence for a divine couple.
  Supports: [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-proves-consort]] (the Arad two steles support the consort reading of Kuntillet Ajrud). Depends on: [[claim-divine-image-prohibition-late-exilic]].

---

## Claim 2: Seals and coins from Judah depict Yhwh in anthropomorphic form
→ [[claim-seals-coins-depict-yhwh-anthropomorphic]] | **romer-inv-8.2** | Confidence: medium-high
  A 7th-century seal (Elishama, son of Gedalyahu) shows a god enthroned. A Persian-era Yehud coin (c. 380 BCE) shows a deity on a winged wheel. The coin's territorial inscription makes the Yhwh identification much more secure than the ambiguous seals.
  Supports: [[claim-divine-image-prohibition-late-exilic]] (the seals pre-date the exile, the coin post-dates it — bracketing the period when the prohibition supposedly emerged).

---

## Claim 3: The prohibition on divine images is late — 6th century BCE at the earliest
→ [[claim-divine-image-prohibition-late-exilic]] | **romer-inv-8.3** | Confidence: high
  The Decalogue's image prohibition is not Mosaic. It was formulated during the exile to distinguish Yahwism from Babylonian idolatry, then projected backward. The golden calf story (Exod 32) was composed to condemn northern bull worship retroactively. Isaiah 19:19 (c. 350-300 BCE) still envisions a *masseba* for Yhwh in Egypt — the prohibition wasn't absolute even in the Persian period.
  Supports: [[claim-josiah-reform-removed-asherah]] (the reform was innovating, not restoring), [[claim-bible-deliberately-obscures-yhwh-asherah-link]] (same retroactive projection pattern).
