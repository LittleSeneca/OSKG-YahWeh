# Römer, The Invention of God — Chapter 2

## The Geographic Origin of Yhwh

---

## Claim 1: Yhwh originated in the southern deserts (Edom/Seir/Teman), not in Canaan
→ [[claim-yhwh-originated-southern-deserts-edom-seir]] | **romer-inv-2.1** | Confidence: medium-high
  Romer evaluates four candidate origins (Ebla, Ugarit, Mari, Soleb) — eliminates three, builds on Soleb (c. 1370 BCE): "Shasu of Yhw(h)." Earliest extrabiblical attestation. Shasu = copper-mining nomads in Edom/Araba. "Might have been a group whose tutelary god was called Yhw."
  Depends on: [[claim-four-biblical-poems-preserve-southern-origin-tradition]], [[claim-bible-acknowledges-yhwh-foreign-origin]]
  Supports: [[claim-yahweh-southern-desert-war-god-origin]], [[claim-yahweh-originated-south-midian-edom]], [[claim-el-original-god-israel-yahweh-imported-later]], [[claim-yhwh-name-means-he-blows-storm-god]], [[claim-yahweh-southern-storm-warrior-origin]]
  Edge: Day (day-ygc-1.2) and Smith (smith-ehg-1.1, smith-obm-7.6) make the same argument from separate evidence.

---

## Claim 2: Four biblical poems preserve an ancient tradition of Yhwh's southern origin
→ [[claim-four-biblical-poems-preserve-southern-origin-tradition]] | **romer-inv-2.2** | Confidence: medium-high
  Deut 33:2, Judg 5:4-5, Ps 68:8-9, and Hab 3:3 all describe Yhwh coming from Edom/Seir/Teman/Paran. Four independent war poems preserving the same tradition. Key vulnerability: dating ranges from 12th century to Hellenistic; names may be mythological (Deborah="bee", Barak="thunderbolt").
  Supports: [[claim-yhwh-originated-southern-deserts-edom-seir]], [[claim-yahweh-southern-desert-war-god-origin]], [[claim-yahweh-originated-south-midian-edom]]
  Edge: Day (day-ygc-1.2) uses the same four poems as evidence for southern origin.

## Cross-Cutting: Chapter 2's Architecture

Römer structures this chapter cleverly. He evaluates four candidate origins and eliminates three before arriving at the fourth. This creates rhetorical momentum — the reader feels the case building. But it's important to note: eliminating Ebla, Ugarit, and Mari as origins doesn't PROVE the Soleb/Sinai origin. It just means it's the best remaining candidate, and the evidence for it is still thin.

| Claim | Confidence | Most Vulnerable To |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| Soleb = earliest Yhwh attestation | HIGH | Misreading of the Egyptian (unlikely — widely accepted) |
| Yhwh originated in the south | MEDIUM-HIGH | Could have been known there without originating there |
| Biblical poems preserve southern origin memory | MEDIUM-HIGH | Late dating of the poems (Persian/Hellenistic) |
| Copper trade as transmission mechanism | MEDIUM | Plausible but speculative |

**Strongest section:** The evaluation of candidate origins — methodical, honest, persuasive.

**Weakest section:** The dating of Judges 5 — Römer acknowledges the full range of scholarly opinion but then proceeds as if an early date is correct. His methodological optimism is on display.
