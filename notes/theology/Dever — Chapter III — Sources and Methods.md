**Key contribution:** Dever's reversal of the normal hierarchy of evidence. For most biblical scholars, the Bible is primary and archaeology is illustrative. For Dever, archaeology is primary and the Bible is supplementary — useful but unreliable, and always needing archaeological corroboration.
