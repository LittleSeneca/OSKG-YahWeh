# Römer, The Invention of God — Chapter 9

## Yhwh and His Asherah

---

## Claim 1: Asherah was a goddess — El's consort at Ugarit — and became Yhwh's consort in Israel
→ [[claim-asherah-el-consort-transferred-to-yahweh]] | **romer-inv-9.1** | Confidence: high
  Majority position. At Ugarit, Asherah was El's consort and mother of the seventy gods. When El and Yhwh merged, she came with the package.
  Contradicts: [[claim-asherah-was-yahwistic-symbol]] (Smith — asherah was just a symbol, not a goddess)
  Supports: [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-proves-consort]], [[claim-josiah-reform-removed-asherah]]
  Supported by: [[claim-ot-asherah-equals-ugaritic-athirat]] (Day — the equation is secure)


## Claim 2: The Bible deliberately obscures the Yhwh-Asherah link by associating her with Baal
→ [[claim-bible-deliberately-obscures-yhwh-asherah-link]] | **romer-inv-9.2** | Confidence: high
  The Baal-Asherah link appears ONLY in Deuteronomistic passages — a deliberate strategy to break the Yhwh-Asherah link. The inscriptions reveal what the Bible edited out.
  Depends on: [[claim-asherah-el-consort-transferred-to-yahweh]] (obscuring only makes sense if there was a real link to hide)
  Supports: [[claim-josiah-reform-removed-asherah]], [[claim-asherah-goddess-in-ot-passages]] (Day — the goddess breaks through despite the editing)
  Challenged by: [[claim-biblical-evidence-insufficient-for-goddess]] (Smith — if biblical evidence is too ambiguous to prove goddess, it's also too ambiguous to prove a cover-up)


## Claim 3: Kuntillet Ajrud and Khirbet el-Qom prove Yhwh and Asherah were paired
→ [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-proves-consort]] | **romer-inv-9.3** | Confidence: high
  The inscriptions from Kuntillet Ajrud and Khirbet el-Qom are unambiguous evidence that Yhwh and Asherah were paired as a divine couple. Not a fringe site — a royal Judean way-station.
  Depends on: [[claim-asherah-el-consort-transferred-to-yahweh]] (consort reading stronger if Asherah was already a goddess)
  Contradicts: [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-symbol-not-goddess]] (Smith — same inscription, opposite conclusion)
  Partially challenged by: [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-cult-object-symbolized-goddess]] (Day — agrees on consort, disagrees on grammar)
  Supports: [[claim-josiah-reform-removed-asherah]] (if the couple was real, Josiah was suppressing a genuine consort)


## Claim 4: What happened to Asherah? Josiah's reform removed her, and the Deuteronomists rewrote her story
→ [[claim-josiah-reform-removed-asherah]] | **romer-inv-9.4** | Confidence: high
  Josiah removed the asherah from the Jerusalem temple (2 Kings 23:6-7). The Deuteronomists then rewrote her story, associating her with Baal to break the Yhwh link. The inscriptions survive as uncensored evidence.
  Depends on: [[claim-kuntillet-ajrud-proves-consort]], [[claim-bible-deliberately-obscures-yhwh-asherah-link]]
  Contradicts: [[claim-asherah-was-yahwistic-symbol]] (Smith — Josiah was removing a symbol, not a goddess)
  Supports: [[claim-asherah-goddess-in-ot-passages]] (Day — suppression only necessary if the threat was real)
  Graham's verdict after three books: cumulative case strongly favors the goddess reading. Yhwh had a consort.
