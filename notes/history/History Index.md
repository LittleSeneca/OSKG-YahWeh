# History Index

Historical and archaeological context for understanding the development of Israelite religion and early Christianity.

## Key Periods

| Period | Approximate Dates | Key Events |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| Late Bronze Age | 1550–1200 BCE | Egyptian hegemony, Canaanite city-states |
| Bronze Age Collapse | ~1200 BCE | System collapse, Sea Peoples, power vacuum |
| Iron Age I | 1200–1000 BCE | Emergence of Israelite highland settlements |
| Iron Age II | 1000–586 BCE | Kingdoms of Israel and Judah, prophetic movement |
| Assyrian Crisis | 722 BCE | Fall of Northern Kingdom, refugee crisis |
| Josiah's Reform | ~622 BCE | Centralization of worship, "discovery" of Deuteronomy |
| Babylonian Exile | 586–539 BCE | Destruction of Jerusalem, birth of radical monotheism |
| Second Temple Period | 539 BCE–70 CE | Persian restoration, Hellenization, apocalypticism |
| Roman Period | 63 BCE onward | Jesus movement, destruction of Second Temple (70 CE) |

## Archaeological Sites of Interest

- **Kuntillet Ajrud** — Yahweh and Asherah inscriptions
- **Tel Arad** — Yahweh shrine with cannabis residue
- **Khirbet el-Qom** — Yahweh and Asherah reference
- **Shiloh** — Early Yahweh cult center (not yet found)
- **Elephantine** — Jewish temple in Egypt
