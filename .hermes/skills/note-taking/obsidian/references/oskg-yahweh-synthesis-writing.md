# OSKG-YahWeh: Synthesis Document Writing Conventions

How to write capstone and synthesis documents from the OSKG-YahWeh claims knowledge graph. Established 2026-07-24 after capstone rewrite.

## Voice

- **Zero first-person statements.** No "I believe," "I am left with," "this means for my faith."
- **Report what the graph reports.** "The graph shows," "the data supports," "Phase 4 identified."
- **No theological conclusions.** The capstone maps evidence. It does not adjudicate what the evidence means for belief. That work belongs to the reader.
- **Neutral, precise, grounded.** Every assertion backed by a claim ID or a phase document reference.

## Wikilink Density

- **Every convergence point** references specific claim IDs: `[[claim-el-was-original-god-israel-name-and-absence-yahweh]]`
- **Every fault line** maps both sides' claims: goddess position claims vs. symbol position claims
- **Every counter-position** references its claims and the stress test results
- **Every structural finding** (hinge, cascade, survival rate) references the phase document that generated it
- **Every key note** referenced inline: `[[Smith Origins — Ch7 — El Yahweh and the Original God of Israel]]`
- Target: 100+ claim wikilinks for a full capstone

## Frontmatter Tags

Super tag-rich. Minimum 15 tags covering:
- Document type: `type/synthesis`, `type/capstone`
- Project: `oskg-yahweh`
- Phase: `phase5` (synthesis phase after 1-4)
- Methods: `claims-analysis`, `knowledge-graph`, `convergence-analysis`, `counter-position-analysis`, `structural-analysis`, `evidence-density`
- Domains: `yahweh-origins`, `el`, `asherah`, `monotheism`, `polytheism`, `divine-council`, `folk-religion`
- Evidence types: `archaeology`, `onomastics`, `text-criticism`, `ugaritic-studies`, `ane-comparative`
- Periods: `exilic-period`, `second-isaiah`
- Methods: `methodology`, `historiography`, `scholarly-consensus`, `counter-positions`

Also include `scholars:` list and `scale:` metadata (books, notes, claims, phases) in frontmatter.

## Structure (for a full capstone)

1. **Pipeline summary** — what each phase produced, method notes
2. **Settled convergences** — each with: claim ID, support count, evidence types, scholars, the Kaufmann dissent pattern. Include convergence candidates that just miss.
3. **Genuine unknowns** — each fault line with: both positions, claim IDs, evidence types, second-order assessment, what would resolve it
4. **Structural architecture** — top hinges with cascade radii, cross-scholar tensions, the Schmid single-point-of-failure
5. **Counter-position landscape** — consolidated stress test results with survival rates and mechanism analysis
6. **Evidence density by domain** — highest/medium/lowest density mapped by evidence type convergence
7. **Graph fragilities** — chronological assumption, Kaufmann instability, Asherah impasse, support asymmetry
8. **Graph at a glance** — condensed reference tables

## Evidence Presentation

- **Every convergence:** support count, contradict count, scholar count, evidence types
- **Every unknown:** both positions mapped, confidence levels, dispute type (same evidence vs. different evidence types)
- **Every structural finding:** exact numbers from the phase documents (not rounded estimates)
- **Tables** for comparison data (fault lines, counter-positions, evidence density)
- **Cross-reference the meta-analysis** for scholarly bias and institutional context

## What NOT to Include

- Personal faith conclusions or "what this means for my belief"
- Sommer/Smith/Heiser strategy analysis (interpretive, not evidentiary)
- "How I read the Bible now" sections
- "The God I'm left with" descriptions
- Any first-person statement about prayer, practice, or religious authority
